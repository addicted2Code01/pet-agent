# Medication Instructions for Luna

## Heartworm Prevention
Luna needs her heartworm prevention pill every month on the 1st.
The pill is located in the small white plastic container on the top shelf of the medicine cabinet in the master bathroom.

## Allergy Medication
If Luna starts itching excessively, give her one dose of Apoquel.
Dosage: 16mg once a day.
Location: The Apoquel bottle is in the same container as the heartworm pill.
